\par

{As a master’s student in Biostatistics-Health Data Science at Brown University, I have gained a strong foundation in statistics and statistical programming and I am still improving on these concepts as the program continues. The academic journey at Brown has equipped me with analytical and technical skills that are essential in addressing most real-world data science problems in the biological sciences and public health fields. The Brown program has reinforced and validated my proficiency in R and the program has extended my use of RStudio towards making professional markdown reports, fine-tuning my development of Shiny apps and taking advantage of all the features offered in the RStudio IDE. I am thrilled to get an opportunity to bring my skillset into a collaborative setting that targets important problems in the genomic field.
An energy transition data science internship at the University of Houston gave me the opportunity to apply advanced statistical techniques in tackling energy transtion problems. In this program I started development of an interactive shiny app for data wrangling and evolved my skills by applying machine learning techniques in making oil well drilling decisions based on data from reputable energy companies.}

\par

{Prior to Brown University and the University of Houston, I earned my MS in Cell and Molecular Biology after spending a year completing PhD rotations at 4 labs in the University of Missouri Kansas City. The lab rotations led to a breaktrhough by unlocking my passion for combining data science and biological data. After completing my MS in Biology, I enjoyed building 2 R packages (\href{https://alphaprime7.github.io/tidyDenovix/}{\textcolor{gray}{tidyDenovix}} and \href{https://alphaprime7.github.io/normfluodbf/}{\textcolor{gray}{normfluodbf}}) that have gathered a good number of downloads from the CRAN repository. One of the packages \href{https://alphaprime7.github.io/normfluodbf/}{\textcolor{gray}{normfluodbf}} was a niche package that facilitated the analysis of data from liposome flux assays which is an assay used for high throughput drug screening in academic and perhaps pharmaceutical milieus. Am I motivated to study science and the pursuit for knowledge? Yes, I believe a fundamental part of being God's creation is a desire to understand the things HE created and so earning my terminal degree in this endeavor is my destiny.
